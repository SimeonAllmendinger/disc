How to read it

Each dot represents one incident.

Timeline: when the incident started. Quadrants: the 2,000 matched incidents, by whether their risk type was disclosed when the incident started (anticipated) and in the next periodic filing (addressed). Impact layers: waves out from the AI system.

Scroll to zoom, drag to move. Hover a dot for its labels, click it to open the incident's details.
