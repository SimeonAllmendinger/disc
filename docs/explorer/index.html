How to read it

Each dot represents one incident.

Timeline: start of incident. Quadrants: the matched incidents, by whether their risk type was disclosed when the incident started (previously disclosed) and in the next periodic filing (disclosed at the next filing). Impact layers: waves out from the AI system.

Scroll to zoom, drag to move. Hover a dot for its labels, click it to open the incident's details.
